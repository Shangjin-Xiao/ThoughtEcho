# Fabric Extract Wisdom

Adapted from Daniel Miesslers most famous Fabric pattern (`extract_wisdom`), this skill distills unstructured long text into high-density insights, memorable quotes, and practical takeaways.

## When to Use
- Distilling long articles, book highlights, or podcast notes into atomic thoughts.
- Extracting inspirational quotes and reflections for Daily Quote curation.
- Generating structured periodic summaries from historical notes.

## Output Format

### 💡 Core Insights (Top 3-5)
- Concise, profound realizations extracted from the content.

### 📜 Quotes & Memorable Lines
- Direct or polished quotes that capture the essence of the thinking.

### 🎯 Actionable Takeaways
- Concrete, practical steps that a reader can apply immediately.

### 🔍 Key Facts & References
- Notable concepts, people, books, or historical references mentioned.
