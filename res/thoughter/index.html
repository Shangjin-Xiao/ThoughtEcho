QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

常见问题解答 Frequently Asked Questions

关于数据安全、计费方式与使用技巧的真实解答。 Clear, honest answers about privacy, billing, and offline use.

Thoughter 会把我的全部笔记上传到云端大模型吗？ Does Thoughter upload all my notes to AI servers? +
不会。 你的全部笔记默认存储在本地 SQLite 数据库中。只有当你向 Thoughter 发起提问、或要求它整理某篇笔记时，心迹才会将当前必要的上下文片段发送给你配置的模型服务商。数据传输采用端到端 HTTPS 加密，心迹官方没有任何中间服务器收集你的笔记。
No. Your notes are stored locally in your SQLite database. Only when you ask Thoughter a question or request a note summary does ThoughtEcho send relevant context snippets directly to your configured provider over HTTPS. ThoughtEcho operates no intermediate server and collects no note data.
使用 Thoughter 需要付费吗？ Is Thoughter free to use? +
心迹应用本身完全免费且开源，没有任何内置广告或强制内购。使用 Thoughter 需要使用你自己的 AI 服务商 API Key（如 DeepSeek 或 OpenAI），调用费用直接由服务商按实际用量结算。若使用 Ollama 或 LM Studio 本地模型，则完全无需费用。
ThoughtEcho itself is completely free and open-source with no ads or subscriptions. Using Thoughter requires your own API key (e.g. DeepSeek or OpenAI), billed directly by the provider based on token usage. Running local models via Ollama or LM Studio is entirely free.
为什么 Thoughter 提议修改笔记时不会直接覆盖？ Why doesn't Thoughter overwrite notes directly? +
这是心迹的核心设计原则——“创作主权永远归属于你”。Thoughter 产生的所有修改与新建均封装为结构化的建议卡片，由你审阅、进入编辑器调整或手动确认采纳，笔记才会发生改变。
This is a core design principle: "Creative sovereignty always belongs to you." All creations and revisions arrive as structured proposal cards that require your review and confirmation before applying to your notebook.
记忆系统是如何运作的？ How does the memory system work? +
Thoughter 拥有专属的偏好记忆，用于记录你的文风偏好与沟通习惯。它在日常对话中逐渐了解你该如何称呼、喜欢怎样的表达风格。如果你想清空记忆，随时可在「心迹设置 → AI 设置」中一键清空重置。
Thoughter maintains a dedicated memory profile for your writing style and phrasing preferences across chats. You can ask Thoughter to forget any detail or clear all memories anytime in Settings › AI Settings.
在没有网络的情况下可以使用 Thoughter 吗？ Can I use Thoughter offline without internet? +
可以。心迹支持配置本地大模型接口（如 Ollama / LM Studio）。只要你在电脑上运行了本地模型，心迹即可在完全断网的环境下与 Thoughter 进行灵感对谈与笔记打捞。
Yes. ThoughtEcho supports local model endpoints like Ollama and LM Studio. With a local model running on your machine, you can chat with Thoughter and explore your notes completely offline.
